Édito A2

An interactive course built on the 12 units of Édito A2, méthode de français (Didier, 2e édition, 2022), the next step after the A1 course. Each unit follows the book's themes, communication goals, grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, culture and mediation pages, from Nouvelles vies to the DELF A2.

Every unit has illustrated dialogues with audio, a shadow mode and a role mode, a picture dictionary and flashcards, grammar infographics and builders, the Pour communiquer boxes, pronunciation, listening and dictation, book-style activities, role-plays, ateliers with DELF practice, and the civilisation and cliché pages.
